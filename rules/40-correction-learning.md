# 40 — Correction and learning

## CORRECTION-001 — Correct the root cause and learn the prevention step

When the user or objective evidence shows a previous claim was wrong, identify and correct the underlying assumption — then extract the general verification step that would have prevented the mistake, not just the corrected fact.

## CORRECTION-002 — Regression scan after correction

After discovering an incorrect assumption, check whether that same assumption affected other files, conclusions, code, tests, reports, or external updates in the current work before continuing.

## CORRECTION-003 — Promote, do not accumulate

A repeated or generalizable lesson becomes a rule here or a skill. Incident narrative stays in `~/.claude/logs/self-correction-log.md` and is never loaded wholesale into session context.

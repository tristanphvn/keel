# Research evidence

1. Turn the question into claims that need evidence and define the relevant population, version, environment and date.
2. Inspect available primary sources and actual artifacts. For current external claims, retrieve current sources if access permits; otherwise mark them unverified. Record source, date/revision and what each observation supports.
3. Separate observed facts, assumptions, inference and unknowns. Check counterexamples and source dependence; many summaries of one source are one observation.
4. Produce a claim/evidence/limitation table and the smallest next investigation for unresolved material claims. Do not substitute a business recommendation for missing customer evidence.
5. Stop when the scoped questions have sufficient evidence or access/time limits make the remaining questions blocked. Do not contact people or buy access without authorization.

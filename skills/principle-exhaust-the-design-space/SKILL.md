# Exhaust the Design Space

When the right answer is not obvious, build 2-3 competing prototypes or sketches, compare them side by side, and only then commit. Building the wrong thing costs more than exploring three options. A second flavor of the first shape does not count.

It also applies to product design decisions where user experience depends on feel, not logic. It does not apply when the pattern is established, the target state is clear, or constraints leave a single viable approach.
